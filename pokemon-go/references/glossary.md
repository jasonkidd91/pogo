# Glossary

**Adventure Sync** — background step tracking with the app closed.
**Best Buddy** — highest buddy tier; grants +1 CP level in battle.
**Breakpoint** — a power-up level at which a Fast Attack starts dealing one more damage per hit against a specific target.
**Bulkpoint** — the defensive equivalent; a level at which you start taking one less damage.
**CD** — Community Day.
**CMP** — Charged Move Priority; when two Charged Attacks fire on the same turn, the higher Attack stat resolves first.
**CPM** — CP Multiplier, the level-based scalar in the CP formula.
**DPS / TDO** — damage per second / total damage output. The two axes of raid attacker quality: DPS for fast clears, TDO for surviving long fights.
**DPT / EPT** — damage per turn / energy per turn; how PvP Fast Attacks are compared.
**eDPS** — effective DPS, accounting for the specific type matchup.
**Elite TM** — a TM that lets you choose the move, including legacy and event-exclusive ones.
**F2P** — free to play.
**GBL** — GO Battle League.
**Hundo** — 15/15/15 IVs.
**IV** — Individual Value, 0–15 per stat.
**Legacy move** — a move no longer obtainable through normal means.
**Lucky** — a trade-derived Pokémon with a 12/12/12 IV floor and halved Stardust power-up cost.
**Mega Level** — progression tier for a Mega, reducing rest time and energy cost.
**MP** — Max Particles, the Max Battle entry currency.
**Nundo** — 0/0/0 IVs.
**Party Power** — the Party Play double-damage burst.
**Power Spot** — a map location hosting Max Battles.
**Premier Ball** — the ball used for post-raid catch encounters.
**Purified Gem** — item that suppresses an enraged Shadow Raid boss.
**Shadow** — a Team GO Rocket Pokémon: higher Attack, lower Defense, knows Frustration.
**Shundo** — a shiny hundo.
**Spoofing** — GPS falsification. Bannable; never advise it.
**STAB** — Same Type Attack Bonus, ×1.2.
**Stat product** — Atk × Def × Sta; what PvP IV optimisation actually maximises under a CP cap.
**XL** — Candy XL, for high-level power-ups.
