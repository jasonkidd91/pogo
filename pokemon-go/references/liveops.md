# Live-ops: what rotates, and how fast

This file describes the **shape** of the event calendar, which is stable. It deliberately contains no
current event names or dates — those live in `snapshot-2026-09.md` and must be looked up.

## Cadence

| Event type | Cadence | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Season** | ~3 months | Spawns, egg pools, research, GBL line-up, bonuses, Adventure Sync rewards |
| **Community Day** | Monthly | One featured species, mass spawns, sharply boosted shiny rate, an **exclusive move on evolution** during the window and for a few hours after |
| **Community Day Classic** | Occasional | Rerun of a past Community Day |
| **Spotlight Hour** | Tuesdays, 18:00–19:00 local | One species plus one bonus (2× XP / Candy / Stardust / transfer Candy) |
| **Raid Hour** | Wednesdays, 18:00–19:00 local | Gyms flood with the current headline boss |
| **Raid Day / Max Battle Day** | Occasional weekends | Concentrated boss, extra free passes, boosted shiny odds |
| **Weekly Challenges** | Weekly | Rotating task set with meaningful rewards |
| **Progression pass** | Monthly + event-specific | Free track plus a paid premium track |
| **GO Fest / GO Tour / Wild Area** | Annual / seasonal | Ticketed global and in-person events; usually the largest content drops |
| **Daily Adventure Incense** | Daily, 15 min | Free; main route to rare regionals and Galarian birds |

## Why the cadence matters for answering

- **A season boundary invalidates**: GBL rank, cup line-up, egg pools, seasonal spawns, seasonal
  bonuses, and rank-up encounters. If the question spans a season boundary, say so.
- **A move rebalance invalidates** every tier list and every "is X good" answer that predates it.
  Rebalances typically land at season start but also mid-season.
- **Exclusive Community Day moves** are the single most consequential recurring mechanic for long-term
  value — if a player asks whether to evolve now or wait, check whether a Community Day for that family
  is upcoming or whether an Elite TM can supply the move.

## Progression passes

The recurring pattern: complete tasks to earn points; a fixed number of points is one rank; milestone
ranks unlock **persistent bonuses that last for the pass's duration** (trading bonuses, raised gift
limits, longer incense, better hatch rewards). A high rank typically grants a Legendary encounter.
A paid **Deluxe** tier adds a parallel reward track without replacing the free one, and is generally
only worth it for players who reliably reach high ranks.

Specific tiers, point thresholds, and bonuses change every pass — **always look these up**.

## Regional and staged rollout

Features frequently ship in stages by region, by Trainer level, or by A/B cohort. "Why don't I have X"
is often a rollout answer rather than a bug or a gate. Check the announcement for rollout language
before telling a player something is available to them.

## Where to check

Current events and season: `pokemongo.com/news`, `pokemongo.com/en/events`, `leekduck.com`.
Current raid rotation: `pokemongohub.net/post/guide/current-go-raids/`.
Never answer "what's happening right now" without one of these.
