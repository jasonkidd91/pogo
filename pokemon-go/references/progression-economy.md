# Progression, economy and social systems

## The four things questions conflate

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Trainer Level** | Your account level, driven by XP. Gates features. |
| **Pokémon Level** | A per-Pokémon investment state, set by Power Ups. Drives CPM. |
| **Candy** | Family-specific resource for evolving and normal power-ups. |
| **Candy XL** | Family-specific resource for high-level power-ups only. |

Raising the Trainer level cap **does not** raise the Pokémon level cap. These are independent.

## Trainer progression

- The Trainer level cap was raised from 50 to **80 on 15 October 2025**, with a full XP-curve rebalance.
- **Levels 71–80 additionally require completing Level-Up Research tasks**, not XP alone.
- The **Pokémon** maximum level was unchanged by that update; Candy XL covers the top of the range.
- **Teams:** at level 5 you join Valor (red, Candela), Mystic (blue, Blanche) or Instinct (yellow,
  Spark). Teams matter for gym control only.

Common level gates to check when a player asks "why can't I do X": gyms, trading, Candy XL, Party Play,
and staged feature rollouts all have level or region requirements. Verify the exact number rather than
quoting one from memory.

## Currencies

| Currency | Source | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| **Stardust** | Catching, hatching, raids, GBL, gym defence | Power-ups, trades, second Charged Attacks |
| **Candy** | Catching, hatching, transferring, Buddy walking | Evolving, power-ups |
| **Candy XL** | Catching, transferring, hatching, trading, Buddy walking; 100 Candy → 1 Candy XL | High-level power-ups |
| **Rare Candy / Rare Candy XL** | Raids, research, GBL | Converts to any species' Candy |
| **PokéCoins** | Max 50/day from gym defence, or purchase | Shop items, storage upgrades |
| **Mega Energy** | Mega Raids, Buddy walking, research | Mega Evolution and Mega Levels |
| **Primal Energy** | Primal Raids | Primal Reversion |
| **Max Particles** | Power Spots, walking | Max Battle entry, Max Move upgrades |

Candy XL becomes obtainable at **Trainer Level 31**. Catching gives a guaranteed Candy XL for
higher-stage and Legendary/Mythical species.

**Stardust advice:** it is the true bottleneck resource. Prioritise a small number of high-value
Pokémon rather than spreading it.

## Items

- **Key Items** (undeletable): Rocket Radars, Mystery Box, Daily Adventure Incense, and any current
  event key items.
- **Evolution items:** Sinnoh Stone, Unova Stone, Sun Stone, King's Rock, Metal Coat, Dragon Scale,
  Up-Grade, and lure-based / location-based / walking-based evolution requirements.
- **TMs:** Fast and Charged TMs reroll at random; **Elite TMs** let you choose, including legacy moves.
- **Daily Adventure Incense:** 15 minutes, granted daily, designed to attract unusual wild Pokémon while
  you move. It is the main route to rare regionals and the Galarian birds. Distinct from ordinary Incense.
- **Lucky Egg** (2× XP) and **Star Piece** (+50% Stardust) — stack these with events for efficiency.

## Eggs and hatching

- Egg distances are 2 / 5 / 7 / 10 / 12 km, hatched by walking with Incubators.
- One free infinite-use Incubator; additional Incubators hatch in parallel.
- **Adventure Sync** records distance with the app closed, feeding egg hatching, Buddy Candy, and
  weekly reward tracking.
- **Egg pools are dynamic and rotate with seasons and events.** Never assume a pool is permanent — the
  in-game Egg screen is the most direct source for the current pool.

## Buddy Adventure

A Buddy earns hearts, improves Mood, appears on the map, earns Candy through walking, and unlocks
souvenirs and perks at higher relationship tiers. **Best Buddy** grants +1 CP level in battle.

Heart limits, Mood mechanics, excited-buddy behaviour, buddy-swap limits and per-species walking
distance all change — verify against the Help Center rather than quoting.

## Research

| Type | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Field Research** | From PokéStops, one task per stop per day; a limited number held at once. Stamps accumulate toward a **Research Breakthrough** (7 stamps). |
| **Special Research** | Persistent multi-part storyline; cannot normally be discarded. |
| **Timed Research** | Expires at a stated deadline. |
| **Level-Up Research** | Required for Trainer levels 71–80. |
| **Sponsored Research** | From partner locations. |
| **Collection Challenges** | Event-linked; often require a Pokémon obtained by a **specific method** (catch, hatch, evolve, raid, trade, Shadow, costume) — a common source of "why doesn't this count" questions. |

## Friendship, gifts and trading

Friendship levels rise once per day per friend through any shared interaction:

| Level | Days | Trade IV floor |
|---|---|---|
| Good Friend | 0 | 1 |
| Great Friend | 7 | 2 |
| Ultra Friend | 30 | 3 |
| Best Friend | 90 | 5 |

Higher friendship gives raid damage bonuses, cheaper trades, and more Premier Balls.

**Gifts** come from PokéStops, are sent to friends, and contain items, Stardust, **7 km Eggs**, and
Postcards. Daily open/send caps exist and are raised by pass milestones.

**Trading:**
- Requires both Trainers to be **Level 10+** and physically within ~100 m.
- Costs Stardust, scaling with friendship and with whether the Pokémon is Legendary, Shiny, or new to
  your Pokédex.
- **Special Trades** (Legendary / Shiny / new-to-dex) are limited per day — verify the current limit.
- Mythical Pokémon generally cannot be traded, and a Pokémon that has already been traded cannot be
  traded again.
- **Trading re-rolls IVs** within the friendship floor above. Never trade a hundo expecting it to survive.
- Trade distance affects the Candy awarded.

**Lucky Pokémon:** a random outcome of trading, with a guaranteed **12/12/12 IV floor** and **half
Stardust** power-up cost. Odds rise with friendship and with how long ago the Pokémon was caught.
**Lucky Friends** — a random chance on interacting with a Best Friend — guarantees the next trade is
Lucky. Do not quote exact Lucky odds without a current source.

## Other social systems

- **Routes** — registered in-game walking paths created by the developer, partners, or Trainers, with
  start and end points and moderation. Completing one grants rewards and badges. Not the same as an
  arbitrary walk.
- **Showcases** — size competitions at designated PokéStops; the biggest specimen of a species wins.
- **Campfire** — companion app for local meetups and Route/Showcase discovery.
- **Adventure Effects** — temporary map-wide bonuses activated by a signature attack on specific
  Legendaries (the Origin Formes, Kyurem formes, Necrozma formes, Crowned Zacian/Zamazenta). **Not
  every signature move has one** — verify the specific Pokémon and move.

## Map and interaction

- PokéStop and gym interaction radius is **80 m** (doubled during COVID and made permanent in
  August 2021). A stop can be re-spun after roughly 5 minutes.
- Spinning yields items, Eggs, Gifts and Field Research.
