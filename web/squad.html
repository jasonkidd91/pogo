Squad

What to power up next, type by type — PvE only, for raids, gyms and Max Battles. Six picks per type, ordered Gigantamax, then Dynamax, then Mega, then a plain attacker, with one slot kept for each so the list stays useful in both Max Battles and ordinary raids. Only one Pokémon per evolution line gets a slot: if its Gigantamax or Dynamax form is already here, its Mega and plain forms are not repeated. Cards are listed in the order worth investing in, and for the two Max tiers that order weighs damage and bulk evenly — so the # on a card is its damage placing on Hub, not its position in this list. Every card opens that Pokémon on Pokémon GO Hub, where the full moveset breakdown lives.

The ranking on this page is Pokémon GO Hub's, not this site's. Every other page here computes its S/A/B/C/D grade from the game's own data file rather than trusting a tier list. This page deliberately does the opposite, for all four tiers, because a squad recommendation is a judgement call and Hub's simulations are the one being borrowed. The two ladders are not the same scale and are never shown together — the chip here is Hub's SS–F, not this site's S–D.

What is still this site's own: which Pokémon actually have a Gigantamax, Dynamax or Mega form. That comes from the generated collection data, so a form Hub ranks but Niantic has not released is dropped rather than recommended.

A Max pick is rated on its Max move damage and its showing on Hub's Max-tank list, weighted evenly — Max Battles cap at four trainers, so soaking hits is worth as much as dealing them. A raid pick is rated on simulated DPS. Those are different numbers measuring different things; the label on each card says which one it is.
