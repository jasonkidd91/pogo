Pokémon GO event

Mega Squads

Mega Beedrill and Mega Houndoom return to raids for the week, and both can be raised to Super Max Mega Level for the first time. Tick them off as you catch them — progress is saved to your Google account and shared with Mega Pokémon. For everything else that is on, see What's on.

    Event: Tuesday 8 – Monday 14 September 2026, 10:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. local time (GO Pass: Mega Squads). Mega Beedrill and Mega Houndoom stay in raids a day longer, through 15 September, per LeekDuck and GO Hub.

    Also this week. Maschiff and Mabosstiff make their Pokémon GO debuts — eggs, Field Research and the GO Pass; evolve Maschiff on 50 Candy. Shiny Flamigo debuts for the first time, in the wild, in eggs, in Field Research and via the GO Pass, all event long. Spotlight Hour is Weedle on Thursday 10 September and Houndour on Sunday 13 September, 6–7 p.m. local both days.

    Starting with this event, Mega Beedrill and Mega Houndoom can increase their Mega Level to Super Max for the first time. Increased Shiny Pidgey encounter rate from Sept 8 10:00 a.m. to Sept 11 10:00 a.m. local time; the Shiny Carvanha rate increases in the second half of the event, from 11 September.
