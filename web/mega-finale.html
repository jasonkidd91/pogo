Mega Finale

Every Mega Raid across both days, with habitat windows, first-time Mega Energy costs and the raid passes you still need. Tick one off as you catch it — progress is saved to your Google account and shared with the rest of the site. For everything else that is on, see What's on.

    Reminders. Signed in, tap any habitat window that has not opened yet to get a push 15 minutes before it does, and another as it opens — delivered by ntfy.sh. Setting one is not enough on its own: subscribe to your topic once on Notifications, or nothing arrives anywhere. (Habitat windows are never more than a day or so out, so the day-before push the events page can send does not apply here.) A window that is open now, or already past, is not tappable. Once the last window closes the page says so and stops offering reminders; your ticks stay as a record of what you caught.
