Times. Almost everything in Pokémon GO runs on local time — a Community Day at 14:00 starts at 14:00 wherever you are. GO Battle League rotations are the exception: they are published as one worldwide moment, so they are marked global and shown converted into your time zone.

Catch trackers are separate pages and only exist where they earn their keep — right now GO Fest 2026: Mega Finale, plus the standing Mega and Dynamax lists. An event without a tracker link simply doesn't have one.
