Mega Pokémon

Every Mega Evolution and Primal Reversion released in Pokémon GO, with first-time Mega Energy cost, the types it boosts for your raid party, and release date. Ticks are saved to your Google account and shared with the rest of the site.

Mega Energy shown is the first-time activation cost. Every later activation costs far less, and drops further as that Pokémon's Mega Level rises. Boosts is the party-wide type bonus — for most Megas it matches their own typing, but Primal Kyogre, Primal Groudon and Mega Rayquaza boost weather-based types instead, marked with a ⛅ pill. The raid pill is the class of battle that species is fought in — Mega Raid (★4), Primal Raid (★5) or Legendary Mega Raid (★6, Legendary and Mythical Megas). It says nothing about whether it is in the raid rotation today, which changes constantly; Super Mega Raids are an event-driven variant and so are not shown here. Super Max marks the extra Charged Attack unlocked at Super Max Mega Level. Megas tagged GO ORIGINAL have no mainline counterpart, so they show base-species artwork.
