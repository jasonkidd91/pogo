A Gigantamax form is tracked separately from its Dynamax entry — Gigantamax Venusaur and Dynamax Venusaur are different catches, so they get their own ticks. Dynamax entries marked G-MAX also have a Gigantamax form on this page. Gigantamax carries a ★6 pill because a Gigantamax encounter is always a six-star Max Battle. Plain Dynamax Pokémon carry no tier on purpose: theirs belongs to the current Power Spot rotation rather than the species, changes weekly, and would be wrong within days — check the in-game Power Spot or a live tracker. Types are left out for the same reason they always have been.
